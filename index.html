Executioner

Found in "The Grotto", inside the "Cathedral" area

Advantage: Increases your Critical Hit Damage by 250%. Disadvantage: Reduces your Melee and Cannon Damage by 30%.

Eradicator

Dropped by the boss "Magnate of the Gong" after his defeat.

Advantage: Eradicator will cause tier 4 Perks to appear. Disadvantage: Lowers your Armor by -30 and your Damage by 30%. Stronger foes will shadow your every step. This perk makes enemies and bosses more powerful.

Health Charm

Location: Looted from Corrupted Treasures.

Advantage: Monsters have a 4% chance to convert their Shards into an Health Elixir. Disadvantage: Enemies drop 15% fewer Shards.

Steel Spine

Location: found in "Corrupted Passageway" in the "The Cathedral". The Corrupted Passageway is the 4th floor of the elevator, using the map to guide yourself, jump down and slightly to the left of the clouds just below the elevator gate and the area will be revealed to you. It is easy to miss the ledges or platforms, so having the "Grappling Hook" can be useful to draw yourself to a ledge as you fall.

Advantage: Increases your Armor by 20. Disadvantage: Reduces your Health and Shield by 30%.

Shield Overdrive

Location: Dropped from "Lith" enemies.

Advantage: Your Shield constantly regenerates. Disadvantage: Lowers Shield Regeneration rate by 80%.
